Research

I study how individual differences in personality traits relate to people's behavior and life outcomes, how they change with age and life events, and how they can be measured. My main focus is on dark traits such as the Dark Triad (Machiavellianism, narcissism, and psychopathy) and sadism (e.g., Shimotsukasa & Oshio, 2021; Shimotsukasa, Suyama, et al., 2019; Shimotsukasa & Oshio, in press), but I also study the Big Five (extraversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness, neuroticism, and openness; e.g., Shimotsukasa et al., 2019; Yoshino et al., 2022). I have also developed psychological scales for measuring these individual differences (list, in Japanese). More recently, alongside this work, I have been studying how a particular individual can be described and understood with reference to findings on individual differences obtained from many people (Mieda & Shimotsukasa, 2025; Shimotsukasa & Mieda, 2026).
