Privacy Policy for "workspace-mcp-personal"

Last updated: November 27, 2026

What this application is

"workspace-mcp-personal" is a private tool used only by Tadahiro Shimotsukasa to work with his own Google account (Gmail, Google Drive, Google Docs, and Google Sheets) from his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What data it accesses

With his authorization, the tool reads and writes email, files, documents, and spreadsheets in his own Google account, and it can create drafts and send email on his instruction.

How the data is used

The data is processed on his computer and by the AI assistant he uses (Anthropic's Claude), only to carry out tasks he asks for. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else. Authorization tokens are stored only on his own computer.

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account page at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
